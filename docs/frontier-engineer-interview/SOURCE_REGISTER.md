## Evidence boundaries

- **Confirmed role/candidate facts:** only the attached skill brief and user-provided context.
- **Public technical guidance:** the linked primary/official/standards sources above. Product features, prices, previews, and APIs can change.
- **Study recommendations:** architecture patterns, labs, question framing, metrics, and story templates created for preparation. They are not claims that the target employer uses a specific product or asks a specific question.
- **Unverified:** exact panel questions, scoring rubric, candidate project outcomes, production AI deployments, savings, certifications, and client approvals.

## Research caveats

The research pass prioritized official documentation, standards, government guidance, and primary engineering sources. YouTube and community resources are listed as optional search paths rather than asserted timestamps because video titles/durations and availability change. The package avoids leaked questions, exam dumps, copyrighted course reproduction, and interview-pass predictions.
